About me

Making something that feels like it belongs there is a puzzle I enjoy, even when it gets tricky. It helps that the documentation is solid and that their developer support actually answers.

Outside monday.com I take on backend and integration work in .NET, and frontend work in Angular. Have a project without monday? Feel free to ask.
